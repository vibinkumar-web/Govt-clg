About YRC

“Youth Red Cross” is one of the wings of the Indian Red Cross Society. It is a movement organized at the college level for students between 18 and 25 years of age. A qualified professor is recognized as the leader and is called the Programme Officer, under whose guidance the students are trained to manage the affairs of the group, electing their own Office-Bearers.

Government Arts and Science College, Sankarankovil established the YRC unit in the academic year 2022–2023. It is a voluntary relief movement whose purpose is to protect life, health, and to ensure respect for human life.

🏥 Indian Red Cross Society

Jean Henry Dunant, also known as the “Father of Red Cross,” was born in Geneva, Switzerland, on May 8, 1828. The Red Cross was founded in Geneva in 1863. It is a non-profit organization dedicated to humanitarian aid. Though it began by focusing on the needs of those impacted by conflict, it eventually expanded its scope to include the prevention and relief of human sufferings during natural disasters and other periods. The most essential component of its parent organization, the Indian Red Cross, is the Youth Red Cross — a student-led initiative for students in colleges and technical schools.

🎯 Objectives of Youth Red Cross

  • To create awareness among students to care for their own health and also others.
  • To promote mutual understanding, friendship, and cooperation among all peoples.
  • To relieve the sufferings of individuals and to give priority to the most urgent cases of distress.
  • To provide an atmosphere towards the all-round development of its members.

🙌 Motto

“Health, Service and Friendship”

🔍 Vision

To be the most effective and dependable institution in this remote area for improving the lives of patients with humanitarian concerns and, as a result, making a man whole by providing unselfish service.

✍ YRC Member Pledge

“I pledge to look after my own and other’s health, to assist the sick and suffering, especially children, and to regard youth from all over the world as my friends.”

Key Activities

🩸

Blood Donation Camp

YRC organises blood grouping camps and donation drives in collaboration with government hospitals, encouraging students to become regular blood donors.

👁

Eye Checkup Camp

Free eye check-up camps are organised for rural communities and college students in partnership with government health departments.

🌿

Tree Saplings Plantation

Regular tree plantation drives are conducted on campus and in surrounding villages to promote environmental awareness and green cover.

🩺

Rally & Training Programmes

Volunteers participate in awareness rallies and training programmes organized by the Indian Red Cross Society, Tamil Nadu State Branch.

🏥

Health & Social Awareness

Programmes on personal hygiene, communicable disease prevention, thalassaemia, and HIV/AIDS awareness are conducted to reduce stigma and spread knowledge.

⛑

Relief Activities

YRC volunteers participate in relief and rehabilitation work during floods, droughts, and other natural calamities alongside district administration.
