About Our College

Est. 2020
Government Institution

Empowering Communities Through Education

Affiliated to Manonmaniam Sundaranar University, the college is located on a 3.31-acre campus on Tiruvengadam Road, Sankarankovil. The permanent Government Arts and Science College building was inaugurated by the Honourable Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu on 22 January 2024.
